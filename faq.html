Good to Know

Questions & Answers

Everything guests usually ask — and if your question isn't here, just reach out to us.

When should I RSVP by?

Please reply by 31 October 2026 through the RSVP page — it takes less than a minute, and it helps us plan your seat and your plate.

What should I wear?

Indian traditional wear across all three days — sarees, lehengas, kurtas or smart traditional wear are warmly encouraged. For the evening: cocktail attire, jewel tones welcome. Please leave white and cream for the couple. Parts of the celebration are outdoors on garden lawns, so choose comfortable shoes.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely — little ones are part of the family! Please include them in your guest count when you RSVP so we can seat everyone comfortably.

What about gifts?

Your presence, your love, and your blessings are the greatest gifts of all. Should you wish to give more, a small envelope is warmly treasured — there will be a place for them at the reception. Please, no boxed gifts — suitcases home are heavy enough!

What's the weather like in December?

December is one of the pleasanter months in Tirupati — warm by day and cool in the evening, with little rain expected. Bring something light for the daytime events and a layer for the evening.

How do I get there, and where do I park?

Tirupati has its own airport and a well-served railway station, and is also reachable by road from Chennai and Bengaluru. Taxis and autos are easy to find locally. More details on the Location page.

Can I share the photos I take?

Yes, please! Upload your favourite moments to our Photo Wall — they appear right on this website for everyone to enjoy, and they become part of our memory of the day.

How do I find my seat at the reception?

Type your name on the Find Your Seat page — your table number will appear closer to the big day.
