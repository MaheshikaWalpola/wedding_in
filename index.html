Our Story ·మా కథ

From two countries to one city, and to each other

I

Two roads

Moksha set out from Tirupati and Maheshika from Colombo, and both roads ended in the same city in Germany: Chemnitz. Different master's degrees, different lecture halls, different circles of friends. On paper there was no reason for their paths to cross.

II

A friendship

Destiny had other ideas, and it chose the most ordinary place of all: a part-time job. Shifts turned into conversations, conversations into a friendship, and the friendship into the steadiest thing in a life far from home. In a country where neither of them had family, the friends each of them made became family to them both, and these two became each other's.

III

A new beginning

Somewhere along the way, without any single dramatic moment, the friendship grew into something more. Two strangers in a foreign city are now two families in two countries, celebrating with two weddings: one in Tirupati and one in Sri Lanka. This is the first page of the next chapter, and we are so glad you are reading it with us.

Traditions ·సంప్రదాయాలు

A short guide to a Telugu wedding

For friends joining us from Sri Lanka, Germany and further afield, here are the rituals you will hear about, in a few lines each.

  1. 01

    Mehendi

    Henna is drawn on the bride's hands and feet the day before. The deeper the colour turns, the deeper the love, they say.

  2. 02

    Haldi · Pasupu

    Family members bless the couple by applying turmeric paste, for glow, for good luck, and for a lot of yellow on everyone's clothes.

  3. 03

    Kanyadanam

    The bride's parents place her hand in the groom's. It is the giving-away, and the most emotional moment of the morning.

  4. 04

    Jeelakarra Bellam

    At the exact muhurtham, the couple place a paste of cumin and jaggery on each other's heads: bitter and sweet, held together for life.

  5. 05

    Mangalya Dharanam

    The groom ties the mangalasutra around the bride's neck with three knots. This is the moment the two are married.

  6. 06

    Talambralu

    The couple shower each other with turmeric-coated rice, again and again, each trying to out-pour the other. Laughter guaranteed.

  7. 07

    Saptapadi

    Seven steps around the sacred fire, one vow for each. It is the walk that turns two people into a family.

  8. 08

    Arundhati Darshanam

    At the very end, the groom shows the bride the star Arundhati in the sky, the old symbol of a marriage that lasts.

Travel & Stay ·ప్రయాణం & బస

Getting to Tirupati

Flying in

  • Chennai (MAA) is the nearest international airport. Tirupati is about three hours away by road, and taxis and buses run all day.
  • Bengaluru (BLR) is the other big international gateway, about five hours by road or an overnight train.
  • Tirupati (TIR) is the local airport, a short drive from town, with domestic connections from most Indian cities.

Tirupati is also a major railway junction with direct trains from Chennai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
