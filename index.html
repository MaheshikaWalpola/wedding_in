Our Story · మా కథ

Fire, water, and what happens when they meet

Fire

Fire and water are not supposed to get along. Everyone knows that. Fire is restless and bright and warms every room it enters — and that was Moksha, long before either of them thought about the other as anything but a friend.

Water

Water is patient. It listens, it holds its shape without needing to be told, and it is far stronger than it looks. That was Maheshika. They met a long way from both their homes, and for a while a friendship was simply that — easy, unhurried, and the best part of most weeks.

What rises

Somewhere along the way, without either of them quite deciding it, the friendship became something else. Not fire putting water out. Not water swallowing fire. Something warmer than either — the way steam rises when the two finally meet. From Colombo and Tirupati to a shared home in Germany, and now back to Tirupati, where two families become one.

Traditions · సంప్రదాయాలు

A short guide to a Telugu wedding

For friends joining us from Sri Lanka, Germany and further afield — the rituals you'll hear about, in a few lines each.

Mehendi

Henna is drawn on the bride's hands and feet the day before. The deeper the colour turns, the deeper the love, they say.

Haldi · Pasupu

Family members bless the couple by applying turmeric paste — for glow, for good luck, and for a lot of yellow on everyone's clothes.

Kanyadanam

The bride's parents place her hand in the groom's — the giving-away, and the most emotional moment of the morning.

Jeelakarra Bellam

At the exact muhurtham, the couple place a paste of cumin and jaggery on each other's heads: bitter and sweet, held together for life.

Mangalya Dharanam

The groom ties the mangalasutra around the bride's neck with three knots — the moment the two are married.

Talambralu

The couple shower each other with turmeric-coated rice, again and again, each trying to out-pour the other. Laughter guaranteed.

Saptapadi

Seven steps around the sacred fire, one vow for each — the walk that turns two people into a family.

Arundhati Darshanam

At the very end, the groom shows the bride the star Arundhati in the sky — the old symbol of a marriage that lasts.

Travel & Stay · ప్రయాణం & బస

Getting to Tirupati

Flying in

  • Chennai (MAA) — the nearest international airport. Tirupati is about three hours away by road; taxis and buses run all day.
  • Bengaluru (BLR) — the other big international gateway, about five hours by road, or an overnight train.
  • Tirupati (TIR) — the local airport, a short drive from town, with domestic connections from most Indian cities.

Tirupati is also a major railway junction with direct trains from Chennai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
